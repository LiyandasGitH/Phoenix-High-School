Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help you get started.

Where is Phoenix Secondary School?

Our campus is on Silverstream Road, Manenberg, Cape Town, 7764. Enter your starting address above the map to view directions on this page.

How can I speak with the school office?

Call (021) 638 4577 or email phoenixsecondaryschool@gmail.com. Please call ahead to check office availability before visiting.

What grades and languages does the school serve?

Phoenix is a dual-medium school for Grades 8 to 12, offering learning in English and Afrikaans.

Where can I find admissions information?

Visit our Admissions page for school registration information, or contact the office with your questions.
