Student Life & Sport

Phoenix learners compete, create, and lead. This page celebrates approved campus sport, culture, and leadership — without publishing private learner records.

Competitive soccer

Soccer is a flagship code at Phoenix Secondary School. Age-group squads compete across the Cape Town school circuit, including documented fixtures against schools such as Wynberg Boys’ High.

U15

Development side focused on match fitness, discipline, and pathway into senior school soccer.

U17

Competitive league fixtures and inter-school friendlies that build squad depth for the first team.

U19

Senior team representing Phoenix in high-profile school matches and community showcases.

Culture, clubs & leadership

Netball

Netball remains a core code for Phoenix, with training and inter-school play that sit alongside soccer in the sport calendar.

Cultural initiatives

Dual-medium identity (English and Afrikaans) shapes assemblies, cultural events, and learner-led performances rooted in Manenberg.

Student leadership

Prefects and learner representative structures support discipline, peer mentoring, and community programmes. Names and photographs are published only with school approval.

German exchange

Selected Grade 11 learners join the Kreisgymnasium Heinsberg partnership (about 15 learners per cycle). Programme detail lives on the Academics page.

Campus gallery

Photography here is limited to approved, consented campus imagery. Families may request removal of a photo by contacting the school office.
