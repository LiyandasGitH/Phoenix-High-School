The curriculum

Phoenix follows the National Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement (CAPS), as set by the Department of Basic Education and administered in the Western Cape by the WCED.

Grades 8 – 9

Senior Phase

Learners follow a broad, compulsory programme across all learning areas. The purpose of these two years is simple: build the reading, writing, mathematical and thinking foundations that make subject choices at the end of Grade 9 possible.

Grade 9 closes with the WCED systemic assessment, alongside the school's own year-end examinations.

Grades 10 – 12

Further Education & Training Phase

Learners narrow to a subject package that leads to the National Senior Certificate (NSC) at the end of Grade 12. Subject choice at the end of Grade 9 is the single most important academic decision a Phoenix learner makes — it determines what they can study after school.

Guidance on subject packages is provided by the school before Grade 10 registration.

Learning areas

Phoenix offers subjects across the standard CAPS learning areas below. The exact subject list for a given year is confirmed at registration.

Languages

English & Afrikaans

Home Language and First Additional Language. Both languages are mediums of instruction at Phoenix, so language teaching is not a side issue here — it is the foundation of the whole curriculum.

Mathematics

Mathematics & Maths Literacy

Learners take either Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy. The choice affects which tertiary courses are open to them, and is made with the school's guidance.

Sciences

Physical & Life Sciences

Science teaching is supported by practical work in the school's science laboratories.

Commerce

Accounting & Business Studies

The commerce stream prepares learners for careers in finance, business and entrepreneurship.

Humanities

Geography & History

History matters in a school built in the 1980s in Manenberg. Our learners study the country they live in with real context to draw on.

Life Orientation

Life Orientation

Compulsory for all learners: life skills, citizenship, careers, health and physical education.

Signature programme

The Kreisgymnasium Heinsberg exchange

Since 2009, Phoenix Secondary School has partnered with the Kreisgymnasium Heinsberg — a secondary school in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany — to send Grade 11 learners abroad and host German learners here in Manenberg. Phoenix is the only African partner school in the Kreisgymnasium's international programme, alongside partners in Greece, Hungary, the USA, Belgium and the Netherlands.

How the exchange works

  • ✓ Alternating visits. One year a German group travels to Cape Town; the next, a Phoenix group travels to Heinsberg.
  • ✓ Home stays. Each learner is paired with a host learner and lives with their family for the duration of the visit — the part past participants say changes them most.
  • ✓ Around 15 Grade 11 learners per cycle. Selection is made by the school; learners who have not repeated a grade are prioritised.
  • ✓ More than a tour. Groups work on a joint project together — painting school walls, building a school garden — and then travel: Cologne, Aachen, Amsterdam and Paris on recent trips.
  • ✓ Funded by the community. Travel costs have historically been around R12 000 per learner. Families fundraise — and so does the school.

How learners are assessed

In the FET phase (Grades 10–12), the NSC is made up of two parts.

25%

School-Based Assessment

Continuous assessment during the year: tests, practical work, projects, oral work and assignments, set and marked by the school. This is where consistent day-to-day work converts into marks.

75%

Final Examinations

The externally set and moderated NSC examination at the end of Grade 12, written in October and November and set by the Department of Basic Education.

Weightings and promotion requirements are set nationally in the CAPS programme of assessment; contact the school office for the specific requirements applicable to a given grade.

Academic support

What we do when a learner struggles.

In class

Teacher support

Subject teachers identify learners who need help and provide additional tasks and revision. Parents are contacted early in the year, not at the end of it.

Careers

Career guidance

Career exposure is built into the school year, including the annual Manenberg career expo hosted at Phoenix with Manenberg SAPS, the WCED and Safe Schools.

Partners

External programmes

Through partnerships with NGOs, community organisations and the WCED, our learners take part in programmes that go beyond the classroom — from motorsport career days to sport and leadership.
