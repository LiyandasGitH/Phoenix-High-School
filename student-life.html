Student Life & Sport

Phoenix learners compete, create, and lead. Celebrating approved campus sport, culture, and student leadership milestones while maintaining strict learner protection under POPIA guidelines.

Competitive Soccer

Soccer is a flagship sports code at Phoenix Secondary School. Age-group squads compete across the Cape Town school circuit, including documented inter-school fixtures against schools such as Wynberg Boys' High.

Junior Code

U15 Football Squad

Development side focused on core match fitness, tactical discipline, and creating a structured pathway into senior school soccer.

Intermediate Code

U17 Football Squad

Competitive league fixtures and regional inter-school friendlies that build technical squad depth for the first team.

First Team

U19 First Team

Senior team representing Phoenix Secondary in high-profile school matches, derby showcases, and community tournaments.

Culture, Clubs & Leadership

Extracurricular activities foster leadership, cultural expression, and international exchange opportunities for our learners.

Girls Sport

Netball League

Netball remains a core sporting code for Phoenix, offering weekly league matches and skill development clinics alongside soccer in the term sports calendar.

Learner Voice

Student Representative Council

Prefects and learner leaders support discipline, peer mentoring, and community initiatives. Names and photography are published with approved media consent.

Signature Academic Exchange

Kreisgymnasium Heinsberg Germany Partnership

Selected Grade 11 learners participate in an international cultural exchange partnership with Kreisgymnasium Heinsberg (\~15 learners per cycle). Program details and application guidelines are available on our Academics page.
