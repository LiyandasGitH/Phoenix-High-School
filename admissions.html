Learner Admissions & Enrollment

Join Phoenix Secondary School. We welcome prospective Grade 8 through Grade 11 learners for the upcoming academic year. Follow our simple step-by-step enrollment process below.

Download Application Pack (PDF) Contact Admissions Office

4-Step Enrollment Process

Follow these steps to complete your child's registration at Phoenix Secondary School

01

Check WCED Dates

Monitor official Western Cape Education Department admission windows and on-site assistance pop-up centre dates.

02

Prepare Documents

Gather all certified learner records, report cards, parent/guardian IDs, and municipal proof of residence.

03

Submit Application

Deliver physical forms to our administration office on Silverstream Road or complete the WCED online portal application.

04

Confirmation & Placement

Receive official registration acknowledgment and placement confirmation from the school administration.

📑 Mandatory Required Documentation

  • ✓ Certified copy of Learner's Birth Certificate or Identity Document
  • ✓ Learner's most recent official Academic Term Report
  • ✓ Certified copy of Parent / Legal Guardian South African ID
  • ✓ Proof of residential address (utility bill or official affidavit)
  • ✓ Official Transfer Card (if transferring from another high school)

🎓 FET Academic Subject Pathways (Grades 10-12)

Science & Mathematics Stream

Pure Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Information Technology (CAT).

Commerce & Business Stream

Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematical Literacy.

Humanities & Social Sciences

History, Geography, Life Orientation, Home Language & First Additional Language.
